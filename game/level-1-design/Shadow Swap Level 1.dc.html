1 WALK & JUMP
TEACHES

Core movement — run flat ground, hop one small gap, step up a ledge. The two twins stand together; nothing forces a swap yet.

SWAP

Not required — this is the baseline the next three beats break.

2 THE SWAP
TEACHES

Press [F] to swap. The gap is too wide to jump and the only span is a shadowOnly bridge.

WHY MANDATORY

The Main falls straight through purple. Only the Shadow can cross to hit switch ①, dropping a solid bridge — then swap back and walk the Main over.

3 SHADOW ROAD
TEACHES

A light source casts a shadow zone up & over a tall wall. Inside the dark, the Shadow can climb where there's no floor.

WHY MANDATORY

The wall stops the Main and the shadow zone pushes it back. Only the Shadow rides the zone to switch ②, opening the gate so the Main can pass.

4 COORDINATE
TEACHES

Hold one twin in place to change the world for the other. The lit stepping path only exists while a body weighs switch ③.

WHY MANDATORY

One twin can't be in two places. Leave the Shadow frozen on the switch, swap to the Main, and jump the lit path to the exit portal.
